For homeowners

Annual maintenance check-up

Most expensive house problems started as cheap ones nobody noticed. A once-a-year walk-through with a prioritized list keeps you ahead of them.

What you get

A 1.5 to 2 hour inspection of the exterior, roof, attic, mechanical systems and the areas of the interior most likely to hide water problems, followed by a short report with three lists: do this now, do this this year, and keep an eye on this. Each item comes with a photo, a plain explanation and a rough cost so you can plan.

The things that quietly cost homeowners the most

  • Grading that has settled toward the house and downspouts that dump at the foundation. The number one cause of wet basements in the GTA.
  • Failed caulking at windows, doors, brick-to-siding joints and tub surrounds.
  • Roof flashing and pipe boots that have cracked or lifted years before the shingles wear out.
  • Bathroom fans venting into the attic, with the frost and mould-like staining that follows.
  • Furnace and A/C neglect: clogged filters, blocked condensate drains, dirty coils.
  • Slow leaks under sinks, behind toilets and at the water heater, found with a moisture meter before the floor is ruined.
  • Deck, railing and stair fasteners loosening with the seasons.
  • Sump pump that hasn't been tested since the last big storm.
Best time to book: spring (after the thaw shows you where the water goes) or fall (before the heating season). Returning clients get a reminder each year.

Who this is for

New owners in their first year who want to understand what they bought. Owners of homes over 20 years old. Anyone who bought without an inspection in a competitive market and wants a baseline. Landlords who want a documented condition record between tenants.

Pricing

Three packages, one standard of inspection

Price depends on the type, size and age of the home, because that's what decides how long a proper inspection takes. Every price includes the full inspection, a thermal imaging scan of the whole home, the walk-through, the report within 24 hours, and phone support afterward. No fuel surcharge, no city surcharge.

Why age matters

  • Older homes have more systems that have been replaced, extended, or patched
  • Knob-and-tube, aluminum wiring, galvanized plumbing and cast iron take time to trace
  • More attic and crawlspace access, more history to read

Why size matters

  • Every outlet, window, fixture and room is checked, so time scales with the house
  • Larger roofs and more exterior elevations
  • Multiple HVAC zones and water heaters are common over 3,000 sq ft

What never changes

  • Same Standards of Practice on every job
  • Same report format, same 24-hour delivery
  • Same inspector, start to finish

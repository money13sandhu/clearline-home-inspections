Local knowledge

What we see a lot of, by area

Brampton, Mississauga and Vaughan subdivisions (1985 to 2010): settled grading and downspouts at the foundation, Kitec plumbing in 1995 to 2007 builds, bathroom fans vented into attics, basement apartments finished without permits, and brick weep holes sealed with caulking.

Toronto and older Etobicoke, York, East York (pre-1960): knob-and-tube and early 60-amp services, galvanized supply lines, clay sewer laterals (sewer scope strongly recommended), cast iron stacks, asbestos-era materials, and additions built over old porches.

Georgetown, Acton, Milton, Caledon and Orangeville: rural and semi-rural properties with wells, septic systems and outbuildings that need their own specialists; older village homes with stone or block foundations; newer estate homes with large roofs and multiple HVAC zones.

Oakville, Burlington and Hamilton: post-war bungalows with additions and second storeys, aluminum wiring in 1965 to 1976 builds, and lakeside moisture and wind exposure on the roof and envelope.

Durham (Pickering to Bowmanville): 1970s and 80s split-levels and back-splits with mixed-era renovations, poly-B plumbing, and older furnaces still in service.

About

Built by someone who builds

Most inspectors learn houses from a textbook. I learned them from the ground up, literally, and that's the difference you'll notice in the report.

M
Your inspector

Manee Sandhu

Before home inspection I spent years on the construction side: hardscape, exterior construction, drainage and grading, fencing, decks and low-voltage systems, running crews across Halton, Peel and the GTA. That work teaches you where water goes, how materials fail, what a proper detail looks like and what a shortcut looks like once it's been painted over.

I started Clearline Home Inspections because I kept seeing buyers inherit problems that were visible on the day they bought, if someone had known what to look for and taken the time to look. I don't do volume. I do one inspection at a time, at the pace the house needs, with you beside me.

How I work

  • Independent. I don't take referral fees from agents, builders or contractors, and I don't sell repairs. The only person I answer to on an inspection is the client.
  • Unhurried. A typical detached home gets 3 hours or more. If it needs 4, it gets 4.
  • Documented. Every finding has a photo and a plain-English explanation of what it is, why it matters, and what to do about it.
  • Available. Evenings, weekends, and the phone call three months later when a contractor tells you something that doesn't sound right.

Our promise

What you can hold us to

We show up prepared

  • Ladder, moisture meter, thermal camera, outlet tester, gas detector, pole camera
  • Listing reviewed and permit history checked where available
  • On time, or you hear from us first

We don't overstate or understate

  • A hairline crack is called a hairline crack
  • A safety issue is called a safety issue, even if it's a $40 fix
  • Cost ranges are honest guides, not scare tactics

We stay reachable

  • Same-day replies to calls and texts
  • Free phone consult on any finding for as long as you own the home
  • Reduced-rate re-inspection after repairs

We respect the property

  • Boot covers indoors, everything put back the way we found it
  • Seller's belongings never moved
  • Home locked and thermostat returned on the way out

Every inspection includes

What's in the box, no matter which service

On site

  • Roof walked when safe, otherwise pole camera and eaves inspection
  • Attic entered where access allows
  • Every accessible outlet tested, every fixture run
  • Furnace and A/C operated (weather permitting)
  • Moisture meter and thermal camera used at suspect areas

The report

  • Delivered within 24 hours, usually same evening
  • Photo of every finding, annotated
  • Summary page: safety, major, monitor, maintenance
  • Age and expected life of major components
  • Mobile-friendly link plus PDF

After

  • Walk-through summary at the end of the inspection
  • Phone support for as long as you own the home
  • Help reading contractor quotes for repairs
  • Re-inspection after repairs at a reduced rate

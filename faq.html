FAQ

Questions buyers and sellers ask

If yours isn't here, call or text and you'll get a straight answer.

Booking and timing

How quickly can you inspect?

Usually within 2 to 4 days, and often within 24 to 48 hours when a condition deadline is tight. Call rather than book online if you need something fast.

How long does an inspection take?

2.5 to 4 hours for a detached home, 2 to 3 for a townhouse, 1.5 to 2 for a condo. Older, larger or heavily finished homes take longer. We block enough time so we never rush the house or you.

Should I attend?

Yes, if you can. Seeing the house with the inspector is the best way to understand it. If you can't make the whole thing, join for the last 30 to 45 minutes for the summary walk-through. If you can't attend at all, we'll walk you through the report by phone.

Can my agent book for me?

Absolutely. Agents book most of our inspections. The report goes to you as the client, and only to whoever you choose to share it with.

Do you inspect in winter?

Yes. Snow can limit roof and grading observations, and A/C units can't be run safely below about 15°C. Those limits are clearly noted in the report, and a return roof visit after the thaw can be arranged.

Scope

What is included?

A visual, non-invasive inspection of all readily accessible systems and components: roof, exterior, structure, electrical, plumbing, heating and cooling, interior, attic and insulation, and garage. See the pre-purchase page for the full list.

What isn't included?

Anything hidden behind finishes, furniture or stored items; mould, asbestos, radon, lead, air quality or pest testing; wells, septic, pools, sprinklers, security systems; code compliance or engineering opinions. When we see signs that a specialist is needed we say so and tell you who to call.

Do you walk on the roof?

When it's safe: dry, not too steep, and the covering can take it. Otherwise it's inspected from the eaves with a ladder and a pole camera, and that's noted in the report.

Do you go in the attic and crawlspace?

Yes, wherever there is safe access and enough clearance. Where there isn't, we inspect from the hatch and note the limitation.

Do you use a thermal camera?

Yes, at every suspect area (below windows, at exterior walls, around plumbing) on every inspection, and as a whole-home scan when the thermal imaging add-on is selected. It's included on every inspection at no extra charge.

Should I get a sewer scope?

On any home over 40 years old, or with large trees near the front lawn, yes. A camera inspection of the main drain costs a few hundred dollars and a lateral replacement costs $10,000 or more. We arrange it with a licensed drain contractor.

Is a home inspection a pass or fail?

No. There's no such thing as a house that passes or fails. The report describes the condition of the house so you can decide what it's worth to you and what to ask for. Every house, including new ones, has findings.

The report

When do I get the report?

Within 24 hours, usually the same evening. Rush delivery within 4 hours is available as an add-on.

What format is it?

A mobile-friendly web link plus a PDF. Every finding has a photo, a plain-English explanation, an urgency rating and a recommendation. There's a one-page summary for your agent and lawyer. See the sample report.

Will you tell me what things cost?

Yes, rough ranges for planning. They're honest guides from current GTA trades pricing, not scare numbers and not lowballs. Get quotes from licensed contractors before relying on them.

Can I call you after with questions?

Any time, for as long as you own the home. Send a contractor's quote and we'll tell you whether it makes sense.

Money

How much does it cost?

Condos from $425, townhouses and semis from $499, detached homes from $525, depending on size and age. Thermal imaging is included on every inspection. See the full pricing page. No hidden fees.

How do I pay?

E-transfer, credit card or debit on the day of the inspection. The report is released once payment is received.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free with 24 hours' notice; $100 inside 24 hours. If the deal falls through before the inspection, just let us know.

Are you insured?

Ask us for current details on errors-and-omissions and general liability coverage when booking, and we'll send the certificate.

Ontario specifics

Are home inspectors licensed in Ontario?

Ontario passed the Home Inspection Act in 2017, but it has not yet been proclaimed into force, so there is no provincial licence today. Inspectors qualify through training and professional associations, and follow those associations' Standards of Practice. Ask any inspector what standard they inspect to and what training they've completed.

What is Tarion and why does it matter for a new build?

Tarion administers the new-home warranty in Ontario. Your pre-delivery inspection form, 30-day form and year-end form are how you claim under it. Deadlines are strict. See our new build page.

What is a status certificate?

For condos, a package from the corporation covering finances, reserve fund, rules and any legal issues. Your lawyer reviews it; we'll read the engineering and reserve fund sections alongside and flag anything that matches what we saw in the building.

Same-week availability across Toronto and the GTA. Evenings and weekends welcome.

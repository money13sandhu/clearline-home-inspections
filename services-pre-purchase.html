Pre-purchase home inspection

The full picture of the house before you commit: what's wrong, what's wearing out, what it will cost, and what's fine. Delivered in time to negotiate.

What we do

A pre-purchase inspection is a visual, non-invasive examination of the readily accessible parts of the home, performed to a published Standards of Practice. Expect 2.5 to 4 hours on site for a typical detached house, longer for larger or older homes. We don't rush to fit a schedule, and we don't leave until every accessible area has been looked at.

You're encouraged to attend. The last 30 to 45 minutes are a walk-through where we show you every finding in person, explain how the house's systems work, and answer questions. Buyers consistently tell us that walk-through is worth as much as the report.

What's inspected

  • Roof: covering, flashing, valleys, penetrations, chimneys, skylights, eavestroughs and downspouts. Walked when safe; otherwise inspected from the eaves and with a pole camera.
  • Exterior: cladding, brick and mortar, trim, caulking, windows and doors, grading and drainage, walkways, driveways, decks, porches, railings, retaining walls.
  • Structure: foundation walls, visible framing, floor systems, signs of settlement, moisture or past repairs.
  • Electrical: service size, panel and breakers, grounding and bonding, wiring type (including aluminum and knob-and-tube), a representative sample of outlets and switches, GFCI/AFCI protection, smoke and CO alarms.
  • Plumbing: supply and drain materials (poly-B, galvanized, cast iron, Kitec flagged), water heater, fixtures and functional flow, visible leaks, sump pump, backwater valve, exterior hose bibs.
  • Heating and cooling: furnace or boiler, A/C or heat pump, age and condition, venting, ducts, filter, thermostat, operated under normal controls where weather allows.
  • Interior: walls, ceilings, floors, windows, doors, stairs and railings, kitchen and bathroom fixtures and moisture checks, evidence of leaks or past water damage.
  • Attic, insulation and ventilation: entered where access allows. Insulation type and depth, ventilation, bathroom and dryer exhaust routing, signs of moisture or mould-like staining.
  • Garage: door and opener safety reverse, fire separation, slab, structure.

What's not included

  • Anything concealed behind finishes, furniture or stored items, or under snow.
  • Mould, asbestos, radon, lead, air quality or pest testing (we flag visual indicators and tell you who to call).
  • Wells, septic systems, pools, spas, sprinkler systems, security systems, appliances beyond basic operation.
  • Code compliance, permits, or engineering opinions. We describe conditions; a structural engineer or licensed trade gives the formal opinion where one is needed.
Tight timeline? In a competitive offer situation we can often inspect within 24 to 48 hours and deliver the report the same evening. Call rather than book online and we'll find the slot.

After the inspection

Your report arrives within 24 hours as a mobile-friendly link and a PDF. The summary page lists safety items, major defects, items to monitor and routine maintenance so your agent and lawyer can see what matters at a glance. If a contractor's quote comes back for a repair, send it over and we'll tell you whether it makes sense.

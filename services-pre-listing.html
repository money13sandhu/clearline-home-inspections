Pre-listing inspection

The buyer is going to inspect your house anyway. Doing it first puts you in control of what gets found, what gets fixed, and how the conversation goes.

Why sellers do this

Most deals that fall apart or get renegotiated do so after the buyer's inspection, usually over something the seller didn't know about. A pre-listing inspection removes the surprise. You get the same full report a buyer would, weeks before listing, while there's still time to fix things on your own terms and at your own price rather than as a credit demanded at the table.

  • Fix the cheap stuff. A missing GFCI, a loose railing or a downspout extension costs almost nothing to correct but reads as neglect in a buyer's report.
  • Decide on the big stuff. If the furnace or roof is near end of life, you can price it in, replace it, or offer a credit, deliberately, rather than reactively.
  • List with confidence. Sharing a clean inspection report with prospective buyers builds trust and can support a firm offer with fewer conditions.
  • No surprises for your agent. Your listing agent can prepare disclosures and answers ahead of showings.

What's included

The same scope as a pre-purchase inspection: roof, exterior, structure, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, interior, attic and garage, to a published Standards of Practice, with a photo-rich report within 24 hours. The report is written for you as the owner, with a prioritized repair list rather than a negotiation list.

Optional re-inspection. After repairs are done we can return, verify the work, and issue an updated report you can hand to buyers. Re-inspection is offered at a reduced rate for pre-listing clients.

Common questions

Do I have to disclose what you find?

In Ontario, sellers must disclose known latent defects that affect safety or habitability. Your agent and lawyer can advise on your specific situation. Most sellers find that knowing is far better than not knowing.

Will buyers still get their own inspection?

Many will, and that's fine. When two reports agree, buyers relax. When your report already shows the issues corrected, there's nothing left to negotiate on.

New build & pre-delivery inspection

New doesn't mean finished. An independent inspector at your PDI, and again before your warranty windows close, makes sure the builder fixes it on their dime, not yours.

Three moments that matter

1. Pre-delivery inspection (PDI)

Before closing, the builder walks you through the home and records deficiencies on the PDI form. It's your one chance to document the condition of the home before you take possession. Builder reps are polite, fast, and not on your side. We attend with you, go slowly, test everything that can be tested, and make sure every item, cosmetic or not, is written down.

2. 30-day warranty form

Within 30 days of possession you can submit a list of defects to Tarion and the builder. This catches things you couldn't see at the PDI, such as issues that only show once you're living there and running the systems. A follow-up inspection in week two or three finds what you haven't noticed yet.

3. Year-end warranty form

Before your first anniversary, a last chance to claim on the one-year warranty for workmanship and materials. After a full cycle of seasons, we look for settling cracks, nail pops, grading that has sunk, window and door seal failures, and HVAC issues.

What we look for in a new home

  • Grading and drainage (the most common expensive issue in new subdivisions)
  • Attic insulation depth and coverage, baffles, exhaust fans actually vented outside
  • Roof flashing, shingle nailing and ridge vents
  • Brick weep holes, caulking, flashing above windows and doors
  • Furnace, HRV and A/C installation and operation; duct connections in the basement
  • Plumbing leaks under every sink and at every fixture; water heater and hot water delivery
  • Electrical: every outlet and switch, GFCI/AFCI, panel labelling
  • Windows and doors: operation, locks, screens, seals, weather stripping
  • Finishes: drywall, paint, trim, flooring, cabinets, countertops, tile, grout
  • Garage door, basement slab, foundation cracks, sump pit and pump
Bring the PDI form and your Agreement of Purchase and Sale. We check what you paid for against what was delivered, including upgrades.

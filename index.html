What's covered

Every accessible system, top to bottom

Roof

  • Shingles, flashing, valleys
  • Vents, chimneys, skylights
  • Eavestroughs and downspouts

Exterior

  • Siding, brick, trim, caulking
  • Grading and drainage
  • Decks, porches, walkways

Structure

  • Foundation walls and cracks
  • Floor and roof framing
  • Signs of movement or moisture

Electrical

  • Panel, breakers, grounding
  • Outlets, GFCI/AFCI, wiring type
  • Aluminum or knob-and-tube flags

Plumbing

  • Supply and drain materials
  • Water heater, fixtures, pressure

Heating & cooling

  • Furnace age, operation, venting
  • A/C or heat pump
  • Ductwork, filters, thermostat

Interior

  • Windows, doors, stairs, railings
  • Walls, ceilings, floors
  • Kitchen and bath moisture checks

Attic, insulation & ventilation

  • Insulation type and depth
  • Attic ventilation and moisture
  • Bathroom and dryer exhaust

FAQ

Common questions

How long does an inspection take?

Usually 2.5 to 4 hours for a detached home, 1.5 to 2 hours for a condo. Older, larger or heavily finished homes take longer. We never rush to fit a schedule.

Should I attend?

Yes, if you can. Walking through with the inspector is the best way to understand the house. If you can't make the whole thing, join for the last 30 to 45 minutes for the summary walk-through.

What isn't included?

A home inspection is visual and non-invasive. We don't open walls, move furniture or stored items, or test for mould, asbestos, radon, pests or wells and septic systems. If we see signs that suggest a specialist is needed, we tell you exactly who to call.

How do I pay?

E-transfer, credit card or debit, payable on the day of the inspection. The report is released once payment is received.
